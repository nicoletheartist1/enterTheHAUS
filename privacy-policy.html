Information we collect

We use information to provide creative, design, web, makeup, jewelry, music, and related services; respond to inquiries; process bookings and payments; deliver projects; communicate about orders or services; improve our websites and offerings; protect our services; comply with legal obligations; and maintain authorized publishing, scheduling, analytics, and account-management integrations.

Facebook and Instagram integrations

When an authorized account connects with theHAUS through Meta's Facebook Login for Business or Instagram tools, we may process the permissions and business-account information needed to manage the connected Facebook Page and Instagram professional account. This can include account identifiers, Page and Instagram profile information, publishing assets, publishing status, engagement or insights data, and other data permitted by the authorization you grant.

We use this information only to operate and improve the authorized theHAUS publishing, content-management, scheduling, and reporting workflows. We do not sell Meta account information. We do not use information obtained through Meta integrations for purposes unrelated to the authorized business functionality. You can revoke an authorization through your Meta account settings or by contacting us.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including service delivery, records, dispute resolution, security, and legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information; object to or limit certain processing; or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, email rnicole@sheisrnicole.com with the subject line “Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.
